What drives us

Our compass

”

To see every individual well-informed on taking care of their mental health and wellbeing, understanding preventive and intervention measures of mental illnesses and psychological challenges — with access to quality, reliable mental health services and psychological support in a professional and trustworthy ambiance.

”

To sustain mental health and wellbeing of individuals through intervention and preventive measures — psychoeducation, mental health awareness, community psychological & psychosocial support, research programs, training, advocating, promoting and supporting mental healthcare systems — while addressing mental illnesses through clinical and psychiatric services.

”

We're always aiming at six priorities:

  1. Promoting & advocating mental healthcare systems in Tanzania
  2. Mental health awareness & psychoeducation
  3. Mental health research
  4. Clinical & psychiatric services — psychotherapy, counselling, screening & assessments
  5. Community psychological & psychosocial support
  6. Consultation, training, coaching & mentorship programs

Our history

The success story of MHT

From a grassroots movement to a national NGO — proudly one of the first and longest-standing mental health organizations in Tanzania.

2014

The Mental Health Movement

MHT began as a movement advocating for mental health — a frontline voice for revolutionizing services across the country.

2018

Mental Health Initiative

The movement grew into an initiative — inspiring agencies, NGOs, government bodies and hundreds of thousands of individuals to champion mental health advocacy.

2019

Registered CBO

Formally registered as a local Community-Based Organization — setting the standard and shifting the narrative on mental health in Tanzania.

2020

National NGO

Ultimately registered as a nationally operating NGO — a pioneering, leading mental healthcare institution serving Tanzania and beyond.
